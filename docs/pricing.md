# Pricing

MFTPlus is priced in four tiers so you only pay for the verification and
compliance depth your team actually needs. No per-server fees, no surprise
overages — flat monthly pricing per workspace.

| Tier | Price | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Community** | Free forever | Evaluating MFTPlus, low-volume transfers |
| **Starter** | $150 / month | Teams that need a verifiable audit chain |
| **Pro** | $499 / month (coming soon) | Compliance-driven teams needing keyed attestation |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Regulated orgs needing anchoring + SSO |
