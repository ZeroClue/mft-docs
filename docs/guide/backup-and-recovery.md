# Backup and Recovery

MFTPlus is a fully managed SaaS service. Backups of your transfer history, job configurations, and audit logs are handled automatically by MFTPlus — no action is required on your part.

You can export your own data at any time from the [MFTPlus dashboard](https://dashboard.mftplus.co.za) or programmatically via the API and `mftctl`.

Interested in self-hosted or dedicated deployments for your organisation? Contact us about Enterprise plans: [support@mftplus.co.za](mailto:support@mftplus.co.za)
